# Privacy Policy — Selran Greenloop

Effective: 2026-06-10

Selran Greenloop runs entirely on your machine, inside your own Claude
session.

- **No data collection.** The plugin does not collect, transmit, store, or
  share any user data, code, or telemetry. It has no backend, no analytics,
  and makes no network calls of its own.
- **Local artifacts only.** Everything Greenloop produces — codemaps
  (`.codemap/`), audit logs (`AUDIT_LOG.md`), specs, test scaffolding — is
  written to your local repository and stays under your control.
- **Your Claude session.** Any code or content processed while using the
  skills is handled by your own Claude subscription under Anthropic's
  privacy terms; Greenloop adds no additional processing or recipients.
